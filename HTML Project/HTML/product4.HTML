Talkboy F/X Drummer

The Talkboy F/X Drummer is one of the most playful and creative tools of the 90s! This pen is not just for writing — it doubles as an air drumstick that plays sound effects as you move it through the air.

With built-in motion sensors and a collection of fun drumming sounds, it’s perfect for anyone who wants to jam on the go. Great for kids, teens, and nostalgic adults alike!
